# 💰 Dynamic Pricing Engine

An AI-powered **Dynamic Pricing Engine** that predicts product demand at different price points and recommends the price that maximizes expected revenue.

The project uses machine learning to understand the relationship between product price, customer demand, competitor prices, seasonality, and other market factors.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The goal of this project is to build a pricing optimization system that can answer:

> **"What price should we charge to maximize expected revenue?"**

Instead of choosing a price manually, the system:

1. Takes current market and product information.
2. Predicts demand using a trained machine learning model.
3. Tests multiple possible prices.
4. Calculates expected revenue for each price.
5. Recommends the price with the highest predicted revenue.

## 💰 Pricing Optimization

After training the demand prediction model, the system evaluates multiple possible prices.

For each candidate price:

```text
Price
   ↓
Predicted Demand
   ↓
Expected Revenue
   ↓
Compare All Prices
   ↓
Recommended Price
```

Expected revenue is calculated as:

```text
Expected Revenue = Price × Predicted Demand
```

The system recommends the candidate price that produces the highest predicted revenue.

## ⚠️ Important Note

The demand predictions are generated by a tree-based XGBoost regression model. Therefore, predicted demand can change abruptly between some price points.

The pricing engine prevents logically invalid negative demand predictions by constraining predicted demand to zero or above.

The recommended price should therefore be interpreted as a **model-based pricing recommendation**, not a guaranteed real-world optimal price.
